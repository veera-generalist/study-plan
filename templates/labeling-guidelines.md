# Labeling guidelines — copy and complete

## Task

Describe exactly what the application is expected to do and which sources are authoritative.

## Examples

Provide a clear pass, a clear fail, an ambiguous case, and the reasoning for each. Include valid alternative phrasings.

## Process

Reviewers label independently before discussion. Record initial labels, source evidence, confidence/ambiguity notes, adjudicated labels, and reasons. Specify who resolves disagreement and when a case is excluded or rewritten.

## Label boundaries

Define material claims, treatment of conflicting documents, partial credit, and whether criteria are mandatory. Record guideline revisions and which records need relabeling.
