# Attributions, provenance, and reuse

## What is included

The course prose, original examples, synthetic lab records, project assignments, templates, and Python lab in this repository were independently written with AI assistance for this study plan. They are not transcriptions of an upstream course. Referenced organizations and maintainers are credited below for their own work.

No upstream course text, notebooks, figures, source files, datasets, or attack collections are bundled in this initial edition. Links are references, not a claim of endorsement or authorship of the linked projects. General concepts are explained in original wording.

All twelve beginner lessons take inspiration from the organization of Microsoft's Generative AI for Beginners: scenario-led explanations, explicit learning goals, examples, assignments, and knowledge checks. The following lesson pages were reviewed on 2026-10-15:

Only the teaching organization informed this rewrite. The Maple Books scenario, facts, prompts, narrative explanations, exercises, comparison replies, and answer explanations were written independently. No Microsoft text, figures, videos, notebook code, or quiz items were imported. Our topic sequence is not Microsoft's lesson sequence.

## Why attribution alone is insufficient for copying

A source credit does not itself grant permission to redistribute. Code, prose, figures, model weights, datasets, and third-party assets within one repository can have different terms. Verify the license for the exact file and revision before importing it.

For any future imported or adapted material:

1. Record its source URL, exact commit/revision, author information, and license.
2. Confirm the intended reuse is permitted.
3. Preserve required copyright, license, and notice text.
4. Identify the local path and describe modifications.
5. Keep source-specific obligations with the material.

Do not assume a repository's code license automatically covers its documentation, external datasets, or model weights. If reuse rights are unclear, link to the source and write an original explanation.
