## Why attribution alone is insufficient for copying

A source credit does not itself grant permission to redistribute. Code, prose, figures, model weights, datasets, and third-party assets within one repository can have different terms. Verify the license for the exact file and revision before importing it.

For any future imported or adapted material:

1. Record its source URL, exact commit/revision, author information, and license.
2. Confirm the intended reuse is permitted.
3. Preserve required copyright, license, and notice text.
4. Identify the local path and describe modifications.
5. Keep source-specific obligations with the material.

Do not assume a repository's code license automatically covers its documentation, external datasets, or model weights. If reuse rights are unclear, link to the source and write an original explanation.
